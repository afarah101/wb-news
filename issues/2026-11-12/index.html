Luqman v Mo Sudz: bromance or breaking point?

Best of enemies. Will it boil over, or will they make up tomorrow?

Western Ballers MediaThursday 12 November 2026Pre-match report

Luqman and Mo Sudz have one of the biggest rivalries at the club. It is part friendship, part feud, and every Thursday it teeters on the edge.

Will it finally tip over the edge this week? Or will the pair be hugging it out by tomorrow? It is all to be seen. The club has asked both of them to keep it on the pitch. Neither has agreed.

Battle of the StrikersForm watch

Forty shots. Three goals. Something doesn't add up.

Last Thursday, Ragz and Hamze A led the line. Between them they fired off 40 shots and came away with just 3 goals. That is one goal for every 13 or so attempts.

40SHOTS
3GOALS
14EXPECTED GOALS

The club's analysts had them down for 14 expected goals. They got 3. That means they left roughly eleven goals out there on the pitch.

Something has to give. The quality of the session cannot be that our strikers score 3 from 40. If the boots stay cold, the club may have no choice but to call up Shamz or TT to play up front. Ragz and Hamze A have been told to find their shooting boots. Shamz and TT have been told to stay warm.

Mr. Ali is backSquad update

Mr. Ali returns to the squad this week. The big question is what form he is coming back in. Has he been training quietly and sharpening up? Or has he spent his time away on the sofa? Nobody knows. We will find out around the second minute of the game, when he either sprints past someone or stops to catch his breath.

Skinz: injured, or just a feather?Injury watch

Skinz has been injured for the past three weeks. This week, the club asked for an update. The update is that he now has a cold.

The club cannot confirm whether the cold is genuine or whether it is something else. Skinz has yet to provide a lateral flow, a doctor's note or a photograph of a tissue. The physio would like to remind everyone that Skinz' earlier injury was in his leg, and a cold is a very different part of the body.

There is also a safety concern. Skinz is, to put it kindly, a slim man. A bad cold, a good sneeze and a bit of wind, and he could be gone. Not injured. Not sick. Just a feather, floating off over the car park. The club has asked that if he does turn up tonight, someone holds on to his ankles at the corner flag.

Verdict:

if Skinz shows up tonight, he's fit. If he sends a message at 19:45 saying he's "feeling rough", the investigation continues.

Last week's recap: Hanad Grove had Mo Sudz in his pocketMatch recap

Mo Sudz was furious last week. Every time he tried to get past Hanad Grove at centre back, Hanad Grove was already there. Mo Sudz tried everything. He tried pace. He tried skill. He tried fouling him, over and over. Nothing worked.

Hanad Grove had him in his pocket. In fact, the only way the club's analysts could explain it was with Prison Break, Season 1. Theodore "T-Bag" Bagwell, behind bars, always kept someone in his pocket. Last Thursday, Hanad Grove did the same with Mo Sudz. We have enclosed a reference photo.

Message to Mo Sudz:

this can't happen again this week. You have a lot of eyes on you.

Advice to Hanad Grove:

when you go home tonight, take your house keys, your car keys, your wallet and your phone. And make sure Mo Sudz is out of your pocket.
