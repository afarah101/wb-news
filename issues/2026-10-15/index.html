INJURY CRISIS DEEPENS AS SIX MISS TONIGHT'S SESSION, BUT RUISLIP RETURNS

Three players make their debut, including neighbourhood baller Saeed Messi, while Chairman Sheikh Ahmed Milky is expected in the stands.

Western Ballers MediaThursday 15 October 2026Pre-match report

Western Ballers FC go into tonight's Thursday session without a host of key players, and the treatment room is now busier than the pitch.

In-form defender Ahmed PB is out with the flu. He has been in tremendous form this season, a rock at the back who has kept his side unbeaten across four games.

Abdulkarim Westway is also ruled out with a calf strain after failing a late fitness test. He will watch from the stands tonight, koob shaax from Baba Salam in hand and Barça jacket zipped to the chin. Is Westway's Barça jacket a statement about his future?

PAPERWORK BLOW FOR ALI AWIL

There was disappointment for Ali Awil, aka Odemwingie, who was set to make his debut this season, but his paperwork was not completed on time. The registration window waits for no one.

Elsewhere, Hanad Grove misses out with a knee injury for the second consecutive week. The club have organised an MRI and booked him in with the best doctor in town, and more updates are expected soon.

Other notable absences include Hamze A, Saf and Adam O. The club hope to have them back soon, but with competition for places this fierce, nobody's spot is guaranteed.

FA IN ATTENDANCE AFTER FORMAL COMPLAINT

Luqman has lodged a formal complaint after last week's session. It concerns players walking off the pitch before the final whistle, and players kicking anything that moves (cough cough, Skinny). The FA will be in the stands tonight. Whether anything changes remains to be seen.

DEBUTS AND A RETURN

In better news, three players make their debut tonight, among them Saeed Messi, the neighbourhood baller himself. Ahmed Ruislip is also back from injury, and tonight's referee has been advised to prepare for an earful.

SUDZ SETS A DEADLINE

Sudz has vowed to bring his Apple Watch and walk off the pitch at 9:45 if his team is winning.

CHAIRMAN IN THE STANDS

Chairman Sheikh Ahmed Milky is rumoured to be in attendance this evening, sparking talk of a busy January window.
